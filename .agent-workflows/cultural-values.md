# Cultural values

Use Spencer's [personal values](references/personal-values.md) when writing or reviewing resume content, cover letters, professional summaries, and interview preparation that discuss motivation, collaboration, judgment, or work style. Read that reference first. It is a statement of chosen standards, not proof that a particular event happened.

## Ground the writing in evidence

- Read the relevant current resume sections and user-provided examples. Choose the one or two values that the evidence actually supports; do not force every value into every artifact.
- Express a value through a concrete action, decision, tradeoff, or result. Keep a private mapping from each proposed claim to its supporting source or user statement so that wording can be checked.
- Separate intentions ("I value...") from historical claims ("I led..."). Never turn an aspiration into an accomplishment, invent metrics, or infer AI safety expertise from general engineering work.
- Preserve the difference between individual ownership and team achievement. Use accurate verbs such as built, maintained, contributed, or led according to the evidence.
- If a useful example is missing, ask a focused question or omit the unsupported claim. Do not fill the gap with a plausible story.

## Apply the values to the artifact

- **Resume:** Prefer evidence of useful outcomes, dependable execution, collaboration, learning, and judgment. Do not add a values section or replace technical skills with adjectives unless requested. Preserve the document's layout and factual scope.
- **Cover letter or summary:** State a specific motivation in Spencer's own voice, supported by relevant work. Present the values as personal commitments without attributing them to another company or leader, or copying an employer's slogans. Use the employer's name only where the application itself calls for it.
- **Interview preparation:** Build prompts from real work: a changed belief, a difficult collaboration, a mistake repaired, a simple solution, or a decision balancing benefit and risk. Leave unanswered prompts for Spencer to supply facts. Practice explaining personal contribution and teammates' contribution separately.
- **Culture review:** Flag contempt toward former employers, coworkers, or direct reports; taking sole credit for shared work; dismissing safety or user impact; hiding uncertainty; and motivation expressed only through money or prestige. Suggest factual, respectful wording. Legitimate criticism should retain its substance; remove personal attacks, not material facts.

## Review before returning

Check that the writing is specific, truthful, respectful, and free of grandiose or borrowed mission language. A reader should be able to understand what Spencer did and why it mattered. Do not treat agreement with a manager as proof of collaboration; constructive disagreement and evidence-driven changes of mind can demonstrate the values.

Follow the repository's normal build and visual-review process when `.tex` content changes. Creating or reviewing this guidance alone does not require a PDF rebuild. This workflow does not authorize submission, publication, or unrelated resume edits; retain the scope and cleanup rules of the invoking task.
